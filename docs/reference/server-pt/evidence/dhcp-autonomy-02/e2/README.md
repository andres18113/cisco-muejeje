# SERVER-PT-DHCP-AUTONOMOUS-02: episode 2

This is the immutable primary archive of the native-pool capacity experiment
on Packet Tracer 9.0.1.0858. `MANIFEST.sha256` hashes every file in `lead/`,
`store/`, `record/` and this README. `store/` is the byte-for-byte campaign
store snapshot after episode 2 closed; it necessarily includes episode 1's
unchanged records because the cumulative index names both episodes.
The four one-use lead scripts retain their exact source bytes with `.txt`
appended to their original `.py` names, marking them as raw historical data
rather than maintained Python. Their original filenames are recovered by
removing that final `.txt` suffix.

The first launch record was refused while Packet Tracer's main-window title
changed between two OS reads. A new read of the same PID and creation time
under the shared lifecycle claim allowed `04b`/`05b` to record that launch.
The first qualification argv accidentally named closed episode 1 and was
refused before phase admission or contact; `07b`/`08b` is the separate,
actual episode-2 invocation. Neither refusal is presented as a native run.

The repeated `setStartIp("192.0.2.100")` produced the exact coupled movement
measured in episode 1. The distinct `setMaxUsers(1)` call then read back the
same `serverPool` with `start=end=192.0.2.100` and `max=1`. Its network and
mask stayed `192.0.2.0/255.255.255.0`; gateway and DNS both stayed
`0.0.0.0`. The process remained disabled and the complete inventory stayed
one pool. These are build-scoped **SUPPORTED_IN_SAMPLE** size observations.
They do not establish gateway, DNS, exclusions, persistence after reapplication,
serving a client, or the maintained product path.

The run used 36 operations and proved fixture restoration. The owned process
exited after its exact save prompt's `No` button was invoked; a later census
found zero Packet Tracer processes, pending mailbox files and campaign locks.
The ledger closed episode 2 at 36 operations and 305.36164 seconds, making
the cumulative campaign totals 70 operations and 652.836009 seconds. The
retirement CLI stdout was observed by the lead but not separately captured;
the indexed `process-exit.json` is the primary retirement record.
